Why Semantic HTML Matters More Than Ever in the AI Era

Published on November 10, 2026 | Category: Web Architecture

Many modern websites rely on deeply nested generic containers that obscure document meaning. However, both assistive screen readers and AI web scrapers depend on explicit semantic landmarks like <main>, <article>, and <nav> to parse content accurately.

When you write valid, semantic HTML first, you guarantee that every human user and automated agent can navigate your information hierarchy effortlessly.
